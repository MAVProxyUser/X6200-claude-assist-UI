NIFOG

CISA's National Interoperability Field Operations Guide{{' version ' + idx['edition'] if idx['edition'] else ''}}: the land mobile radio channels agencies share in disasters (the national interoperability and calling channels on VHF, UHF, 700 and 800 MHz), CTCSS and NAC codes, marine, aviation, HF and amateur emergency frequencies, and the references around them. The PDF here is CISA's own, unchanged. The radio shows the same pages on the Sentry's F > NIFOG, without a network.
