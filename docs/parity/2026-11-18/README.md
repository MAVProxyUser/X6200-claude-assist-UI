# The X6100 and the X6200 side by side, 2026-11-18

A newer run, after the fixes these pictures led to: [../2026-11-19](../2026-11-19/README.md).

Every screen of both radios at the same moment, from `tools/bench/suite.py run core`: each app the radios have
(the remote's `apps`, opened with `open N`) and every page of the Sentry, photographed on both over SSH and put
side by side, the X6100 on the left. Both radios ran the same GUI build (main at 1c9d9bb, hot-installed; the
X6100's own driver), the Sentry theme, the X6100's look-and-behaviour settings set as the X6200's
(`suite.py align`). The suite: 56 pass, RADE to look at (it opens only with Bluetooth headphones or TCI, so it
is not here), the Modes menu's check fixed after (not here either).

Each picture's colours are counted against the Sentry theme's gold on black: **cool** is cyan, blue or purple,
what a screen still in an old theme shows (flagged over 1 %); the warm gradient (the waterfall's dark orange to
gold) and green status words (D's OPEN) are the theme's own. Every screen here is 0 % cool on both radios but
Field Ops (0.4 % and 0.6 %: the colours of its tiles' icons), under the flag.

**Redacted:** what would place the station (its position, its locator, the networks and stations around it) is
pixelated or flattened in place, on both radios alike; the screens it touches say what. The radios' differences
seen here, and what the run found, are in [TODO.md](../../../TODO.md) item 12.
